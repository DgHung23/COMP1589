Current Situation

Our environment is under pressure from pollution, disappearing green spaces, and climate-related damage. These are urgent problems, but they are also problems a strong community can respond to.

Plastic waste growth

Streets, canals, and public parks are still facing rising levels of disposable waste that threaten both beauty and public health.
